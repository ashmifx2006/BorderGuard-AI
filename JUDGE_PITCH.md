# BorderGuard AI — Judge Pitch

## 30-second pitch

BorderGuard AI is an **explainable border-event intelligence platform** that turns raw CCTV detections into context-aware, prioritized security incidents. Instead of replacing existing cameras, it adds an intelligence layer: YOLO detects objects, tracking establishes movement, virtual zones and temporal rules identify events, a transparent 0–100 risk model prioritizes them, and the system captures evidence for a human officer to verify and resolve.

## Why it is different

This is not another bounding-box CCTV demo. The innovation is the layer between detection and decision: **context, correlation, explainability, evidence, and human verification**.

## Demo sentence

"Watch the same person go from detection, to tracking, to a restricted-zone event, to contextual risk, to an explainable alert, then let the officer verify the evidence and resolve the incident."

## Safety statement

The prototype is decision support. It flags measurable video events; it does not claim to determine criminal intent or identify people.
